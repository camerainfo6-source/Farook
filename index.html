WHO WE ARE

Comfort is not a luxury.
It is everyday care.

Comfort Girls is a sanitary pad brand focused on making period care accessible, dependable and comfortable.

Our business operates with a manufacturing head office in Delhi and a distributor branch in Dehri On Sone, Rohtas, Bihar. We work to build trusted relationships with retailers, wholesalers, institutions and distribution partners.

Our goal is simple: offer thoughtfully developed feminine hygiene products and make them easier to access through a growing distribution network.
